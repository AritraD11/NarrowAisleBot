Correction applied by SLAM

How far SLAM has moved the estimate away from raw wheel odometry, over the run. Flat means the wheels were trusted. A step means the pose graph pulled. Wheel odometry cannot jump, so a step here with normal wheel motion means the graph moved and the robot did not.

Wheels — commanded vs actual

One panel per wheel rather than eight lines on one axis. Amber is what the controller asked for, blue is what the encoder reported. Persistent gaps mean the wheel is not doing what it was told, and odometry is computed from all four.

Distance each wheel rolled

Encoder arc length per wheel. On a flat lap these should be comparable; one wheel well below the others is slip or a mechanical fault, and it corrupts the map through the pose estimate.
