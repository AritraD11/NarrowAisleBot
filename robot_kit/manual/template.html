A blank SD card to a robot that drives from a phone, logs its encoders, tunes its own PID, maps a room and takes navigation goals.

Built from the NarrowAisleBot project (Aritra Das, IIT Bombay), rewritten so the motors, encoders, microcontroller and drive type are inputs you provide rather than things you inherit.
